NEUROBRIDGE DIGITAL THERAPY PLATFORM

Project Proposal

Target Completion: End of Quarter 2 (Jun e)



1. Executive Summary

Neurobridge is a hybrid digital therapy platform designed to support children with special needs through structured in-person and virtual therapy services.
The platform enables parents to book sessions, access therapy resources, schedule follow-ups, and make secure payments. Therapists will manage appointments, track progress, and provide digital content.
Initial launch will focus on Ghana with scalability for international expansion.
